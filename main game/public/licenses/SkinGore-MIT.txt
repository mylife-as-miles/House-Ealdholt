SkinGore by zulubo
https://github.com/zulubo/SkinGore (commit e21aeaf809f97518af788093291910d9239dde74)

Crownfall's wound masks (web/src/scene/gore/WoundMasks.ts, woundMaskShader.ts and
woundMaskPlan.ts) port its technique to three.js: damage painted on the GPU into a
small texture in the skinned mesh's UV space, dilated across the seams, added into
a texture the character keeps, and shown by its material. No textures, demo scenes
or other assets from the repository are used. Its licence:
